🛡️ Privacy & Your Data

Before you give consent below, please read how this data is handled. This notice is written in plain language so students and parents both understand.

What we collect & why
  • Identity & contact: name, date of birth, email, phone — to register the volunteer and contact them about the event.
  • Parent/guardian info: required because volunteers are minors. Used for consent and emergency contact only.
  • School & grade: so GTA knows where their volunteers come from.
  • Volunteer role & notes: to plan shifts and match interests.
  • Medical info (optional): used only by event-day staff for safety. Never sent in confirmation emails. Never shared with the AI assistant.
Where the data goes
  • A private Google Sheet only GTA volunteer coordinators can see.
  • A confirmation email to you and your parent (no medical info included).
  • An AI assistant (Groq) may briefly process your name, school, role, and notes to write a personalized welcome and certificate. Medical info is never sent to the AI.
  • Submissions over the internet are encrypted (HTTPS).
How long we keep it & your rights
  • Records are kept for 60 days after the event, then archived or deleted.
  • You can delete your registration any time using the "Delete my registration" link in your confirmation email.
  • Parents can revoke consent at any time by emailing the GTA team.
For volunteers under 13

This form requires parent or guardian consent, in line with US child-privacy guidelines (COPPA). The form must be filled out together with a parent or legal guardian. By checking the consent box below, the parent confirms they have read this notice and give permission.
